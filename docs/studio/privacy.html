Privacy & measurement

This free studio preview is operated by Hazim Alsalmi. Contact: hamammalsalmy9@gmail.com.

Your brief

The form prepares text in your browser. It has no backend and does not send your brief automatically. Selecting “Open email app” passes the draft to your chosen email application; you review and send it there. Do not include passwords, access tokens, personal IDs or confidential client files.

Optional local counts

Measurement is off by default. If enabled, this browser stores counts of work links, service choices, brief previews, email-open clicks and brief downloads. No names, email addresses, brief contents, visitor identifiers or session recordings are captured. Counts are not sent to us and do not measure site-wide traffic, qualified leads or payments. You can export or clear them in the footer; turning measurement off clears this site's local counts.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages serves these public files and may process standard hosting request data under GitHub's policies. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. External GitHub, LinkedIn and email links follow their own providers' policies. No external analytics script is loaded.

Business evidence

Concept work is labelled. Synthetic dashboard values are not customer results. Before a real project, data access, retention, source formats and acceptance criteria are agreed separately. Marketplace inquiries and payments remain on their originating platform.
