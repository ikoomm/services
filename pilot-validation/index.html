What each stage means

Shortlisted
Potential fit only. No message sent; audience and permission review still required.
Contacted
Approved first message sent and dated. A follow-up does not add another prospect.
Responded
A reply arrived. Record intent in notes; a reply alone is not participation.
Test scheduled
A session date is booked. Keep completion blank until the agreed test actually happens.
Test completed
The agreed prototype task was attempted and its outcome recorded, even if the task failed.
Feedback received
A feedback record was received and linked by prospect ID. A request for feedback does not count.
Opted out
Stop further outreach. Record the withdrawal and preserve only the minimum suppression record.

A practical first week

  1. Day 1 · Agree the pilotConfirm the audience, cities, prototype tasks, approved message, contact rules, feedback permission and what counts as a completed test.
  2. Day 2 · Prepare and reviewBuild a small relevant shortlist with source links. Deduplicate and check the suppression list. Get the client’s approval before outreach.
  3. Days 3–4 · Invite and scheduleSend approved individual invitations through agreed channels. Log replies, opt-outs and actual bookings. Follow up only within the agreed cadence.
  4. Days 4–5 · Test and learnRun available self-guided or agreed live sessions; record the prototype version, task outcome and observed friction. Request structured feedback with permission.
  5. Day 7 · Review the evidenceReport each denominator, unresolved sessions and recurring feedback themes. Agree whether weeks 2–4 should continue, change audience or refine the prototype.

Sequence adapts to availability. No guaranteed participant count or response rate.

Before using this with real people

Approve first. Replace all fictional records. Use a client-approved audience, source list, message and outreach channel; establish the appropriate permission to contact. A public source URL is not consent.

Respect opt-outs. Offer a clear way to decline. Stop immediately after withdrawal. Do not enter personal emails, phone numbers, sensitive details or private recordings in this shared tracker; keep any necessary contact data in an approved access-controlled system.

Keep evidence precise. In the feedback log use task outcomes such as completed, blocked or not attempted. Mark suggested explanations as hypotheses. Obtain permission for quotes or recordings, and agree access and retention before collecting real feedback.
