How records are handled

IDs are exact, case-sensitive text: 001 and 1 are different. Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD or ISO timestamps with Z or an explicit time-zone offset. A date without a time is compared at midnight UTC.

New IDs are added. Newer records replace the entire master row, including blank values. Records absent from the export stay in the master; nothing is deleted. Repeated incoming IDs use the newest valid date. Conflicting values at the newest date are held for review, not chosen arbitrarily.

Repeated master IDs stop the comparison. Master rows with missing IDs or invalid dates stay untouched. Invalid incoming rows are skipped and logged. The file row numbers in logs count CSV records, including the header; a quoted multiline cell is one record.

Privacy, limits & spreadsheet safety

Files are read in this browser tab and are never uploaded. This tool has no network requests, accounts, storage or analytics. Reload or clear to discard the data. Work from copies and review the output before using it in a production workflow.

CSV only: comma separators, UTF-8 encoding, one header row, maximum 5 MB and 50,000 data rows combined. Excel workbooks, semicolon-delimited files, fuzzy matching and large payroll workflows are outside this demo.

For spreadsheet safety, exported cells that could begin a formula are prefixed with an apostrophe. This deliberately also protects values beginning with a minus sign, including negative numbers. A protected value can therefore differ from the raw input. Headers are protected too. Confirm your downstream import rules before reusing an export.
